2. Strong-mayor framework

Thunder Bay is subject to Ontario's strong-mayor framework. This is particularly important because we are developing a platform for a councillor, rather than a mayoral candidate.

Ontario — Strong Mayor Powers and Duties

Ontario Regulation 530/22 — Strong Mayor Powers

Ontario's official material explains the additional powers and responsibilities of the head of council, including the municipal budget process and certain powers relating to bylaws and provincial-municipal priorities.

Campaign implication: We should be careful with wording such as "As your councillor, I will..." versus "As Mayor, I would...". You are running for one seat on Council, and your influence comes through Council's decision-making, your vote, committee work, representation, policy development and oversight.
